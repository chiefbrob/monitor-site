Baby Monitor for Android · version 0.6.0

The first build is being prepared for Google Play. These are the questions the prototype is designed to answer — and the limits you should know before you trust it overnight.

Does the app record or upload audio?

No. It reads short microphone frames, computes a loudness value, and throws the frames away. There is no recorder, no audio file, no cloud and no analytics SDK. Read the privacy policy.

Why does Android show a permanent notification?

Android requires a visible notification for any app that keeps the microphone open in the background. Without it the system would stop the monitor. The notification also has a Stop button.

What does −40 dBFS mean?

dBFS is loudness relative to the loudest signal the phone can represent; 0 dBFS is the maximum and quieter sounds are negative. −40 dBFS is roughly a loud voice across a quiet room on a typical phone. The absolute number varies by device, so use Learn room to set it from your own room, then adjust by ear.

The alert appeared but no call was placed.

Check three things: the phone number is valid and includes the country code; the Phone permission is allowed; and the app has full-screen alert access (Android 14+). If calling permission is missing, the alert opens the dialer with the number filled in — tap the call button there.

Will it keep listening with the screen off?

Yes, while the foreground service is running. Android’s battery optimisation can still stop it, so allow unrestricted battery use for the app. Keep the phone plugged in for overnight monitoring. No app can guarantee delivery through every manufacturer’s battery saver.

Does it need internet?

The monitor, alert and call work offline. The free banner ad needs internet; if you are offline, the monitor keeps working and the ad stays blank. The call itself uses your mobile network, as any call does.

Can I watch from another phone?

Yes, on the same Wi-Fi. Turn on Live view on the monitor phone, open Watch on the second phone and pick the room. The phones connect to each other directly, with no account, no pairing code and no cloud. Audio is always shared; video is optional and the camera runs only while someone is watching. Guest networks and routers that isolate devices will block it.

Is this a medical device?

No. It is an audio aid, not a medical device, and not a substitute for adult supervision. It detects loudness, not breathing, movement or wellbeing.

How do I stop it?

Open the app and press Stop monitoring, or use the Stop action on the ongoing notification. Uninstalling also stops it and deletes the number and settings.

Report a problem

Include your phone model, Android version, and what the alert did (rang, showed a notification, opened the dialer, or nothing). Do not send a recording of your room — the app does not create one, and we do not want one.
