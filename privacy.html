Privacy policy

Monitor for Android · Effective 12 November 2026 · Version 1.1

Monitor (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a noise monitor for Android, published by Dabotap. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app listens, decides, and calls — all on your phone. Audio is never recorded and never sent anywhere.

1. Who we are

Dabotap, Nairobi, Kenya. Contact for privacy questions: brian@dabotap.com. The app is distributed through Google Play under the package name com.dabotap.monitor.

2. What the app does

The app keeps the phone’s microphone open while you have monitoring turned on. It converts short audio frames into a loudness reading, compares that reading with the threshold you chose, and — when the threshold is crossed for long enough — shows an alert and can place a phone call to the number you entered.

3. Audio

  • We do not record audio. No audio file, clip or buffer is written to storage.
  • We do not transmit audio. The app has no backend, no cloud storage and no audio networking code.
  • Audio is processed in memory long enough to calculate a loudness value (dBFS), then discarded.
  • The microphone is used only while monitoring is on. When you press Stop, or Android stops the service, the microphone is released.

4. The phone number you enter

The number is stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is used for one purpose: to place the call you asked for when an alert fires. It is not sent to us, sold, or shared with anyone. If you never enter a number, the alert still works; it simply cannot call.

5. Settings and event history

The app stores your threshold, hold time, cooldown, auto-call and vibrate preferences, plus the last alert time and whether monitoring was on. This is local app data, visible only to the app (and to someone with access to your unlocked device or a device backup, if you enable one).

6. Permissions and why they exist

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneTo measure room loudness while monitoring is on.
PhoneTo place the call to the number you set. If you decline, the alert opens the dialer with the number filled in instead.
NotificationsAndroid requires a visible notification for a foreground service; it also carries alerts.
Foreground service (microphone)To keep listening while the screen is off or the app is in the background.
Full-screen alertsTo show the alert over the lock screen. On Android 14+ this is special access; without it the alert appears as a high-priority notification.
Wake lock, vibrateTo keep the CPU alive during monitoring and to make the alert noticeable.

7. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, photos or files.
  • No device identifiers transmitted to us.
  • No audio, transcripts or recordings.

8. Sharing

We do not share app data with third parties, because we do not receive it. Google Play itself processes install and crash information under Google’s own privacy policy; that is between you and Google.

9. Retention and deletion

Your settings and number remain on your phone until you change them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage. We hold no copy to delete.

10. Security

Data stays inside the app’s sandbox. The best protection is your device lock and keeping Android up to date. A call placed by the app goes through your phone’s normal telephony stack and your mobile carrier, which have their own policies.

11. Children

Monitor is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not record audio; it only measures loudness in the room.

12. Your choices

  • Stop monitoring at any time from the app or its notification.
  • Revoke microphone, phone or notification access in Android Settings.
  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to delete the number and settings.

13. Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page and the in-app information will be updated before the change ships, and the effective date above will move. The current version will always be at monitor.dabotap.com/privacy.

14. Contact

Questions, corrections or a deletion request (though we hold nothing to delete): brian@dabotap.com.
