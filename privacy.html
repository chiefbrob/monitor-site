Privacy policy

Baby Monitor for Android · Effective 12 November 2026 · Version 1.4

Baby Monitor (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a noise monitor for Android, published by Dabotap. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app listens, decides, and calls — all on your phone. Audio is never recorded and never reaches us. If you turn on Live view, the room’s audio and (optionally) video go directly to another device on your own Wi-Fi. If you turn on Remote photos, a single photo at a time can be requested by the phone you paired, over your own Tailscale network. We never receive your audio, video or photos.

1. Who we are

Dabotap, Nairobi, Kenya. Contact for privacy questions: brian@dabotap.com. The app is distributed through Google Play under the package name com.dabotap.monitor.

2. What the app does

The app keeps the phone’s microphone open while you have monitoring turned on. It converts short audio frames into a loudness reading, compares that reading with the threshold you chose, and — when the threshold is crossed for long enough — shows an alert and can place a phone call to the number you entered.

Live view is an optional second screen. When you turn it on, this phone advertises itself on the local network and a second phone running the same app can find it and watch or listen. Only devices on the same Wi-Fi can connect, and the connection is direct: there is no relay server, no account and no internet upload.

Remote photos is a separate, optional away-from-home feature. When you turn it on and pair one phone, that phone can ask this camera for a single photo over your own Tailscale network. It is photos only: there is no remote live video or audio. Dabotap runs no relay and receives no copy.

3. Audio, video, Live view and Remote photos

  • We do not record audio or video. No audio file, clip, frame or buffer is written to storage, and there is no recording feature.
  • Audio is processed in memory long enough to calculate a loudness value (dBFS), then discarded.
  • The microphone is used only while monitoring is on. When you press Stop, or Android stops the service, the microphone is released.
  • Live view is local only. If you turn it on, the microphone track — and the camera while a viewer is connected — is sent directly to the other device on your Wi-Fi, encrypted in transit. The audio and video never pass through a Dabotap server, a cloud service or the internet.
  • The camera is used only while someone is watching. The camera starts when a viewer connects and stops when the last one leaves. It is released with the service.
  • Live view is off by default and is not needed for the noise monitor, the alert or the call.
  • Remote photos are photos only. Each request opens the camera for one capture, returns one JPEG to the paired phone, and closes the camera. No photo is written to storage by the app, and no photo is sent to Dabotap. The camera will not take a photo while a live viewer is using it.
  • Remote photos are off by default. They need the free Tailscale app on both phones. Tailscale carries the encrypted traffic between your devices; Dabotap is not part of that network.
  • Pairing is a code you can revoke. The camera shows a QR code containing its tailnet address and a long random token. The token is stored encrypted on both phones. New code invalidates the old pairing; Forget this camera removes it from the viewer.

4. The phone number you enter

The number is stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is used for one purpose: to place the call you asked for when an alert fires. It is not sent to us, sold, or shared with anyone. If you never enter a number, the alert still works; it simply cannot call.

5. Settings and event history

The app stores your threshold, hold time, cooldown and vibrate preferences, plus the last alert time and whether monitoring was on. This is local app data, visible only to the app (and to someone with access to your unlocked device or a device backup, if you enable one).

7. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.
  • No audio, transcripts or recordings.
  • No account, contacts, calendar or files.
  • No audio, video or photo uploaded to us: Live view is a direct connection between two devices on your Wi-Fi, and remote photos travel directly between your two phones over your Tailscale network.

Advertising

The app is free and shows one banner ad supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interactions, and may share that data with Google and its ad partners for advertising. Audio is never sent to AdMob or any advertiser. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows a Google consent form before requesting ads. You can decline: the monitor still works and the banner simply does not load. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Sharing

We do not receive or share your audio, number, settings or photos. If you turn on Remote photos, the photo goes to the phone you paired through Tailscale, a third-party private network you install and sign into. Tailscale carries the encrypted traffic and may process connection metadata (which of your devices connected, when, and how much data) under Tailscale’s privacy policy; it cannot read the photo, and Dabotap receives nothing. The banner ad is served by Google AdMob, which may collect and share the advertising data described above under Google’s privacy policy. Google Play also processes install and crash information under its own policy. Those are between you and Google.

9. Retention and deletion

Your settings and number remain on your phone until you change them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage. We hold no copy to delete. Live view streams exist only for the moment they are watched: nothing is stored by the app, and we never receive a copy. Remote photos are held in memory only for the HTTP response: the app writes no photo to disk and keeps no history, and we never receive a copy.

10. Security

Data stays inside the app’s sandbox. The best protection is your device lock and keeping Android up to date. A call placed by the app goes through your phone’s normal telephony stack and your mobile carrier, which have their own policies. Remote-photo pairing tokens are stored with a key held by Android Keystore, and the camera endpoint requires that token and accepts only local-network or tailnet peers. Photos are carried by WireGuard (Tailscale) rather than plain internet, so the app allows cleartext HTTP only inside that tunnel.

11. Children

Baby Monitor is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not record audio; it only measures loudness in the room.

12. Your choices

  • Stop monitoring at any time from the app or its notification.
  • Revoke microphone, phone or notification access in Android Settings.
  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to delete the number and settings.
  • Decline the ad consent form where it is shown; the banner will not load.
  • Turn Live view off, or press Stop while watching, to end a local stream immediately.
  • Turn Remote photos off, or press New code on the camera phone, to revoke the paired phone; press Forget this camera on the viewer to remove your half.

13. Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page and the in-app information will be updated before the change ships, and the effective date above will move. The current version will always be at monitor.dabotap.com/privacy.
